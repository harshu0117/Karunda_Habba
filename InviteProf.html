It is our pleasure to invite you to Karunada Habba, an evening celebrating Karnataka's cultural heritage and the traditions of Mysuru Dasara with the IITGN community.

The programme will open with the traditional lighting of the lamp by faculty members, followed by a welcome song and a variety of cultural events. The evening will feature traditional dance, songs, instrumental music, poetry, etc. concluding with a festival meal.
  We would be honoured by your presence and, if you are available, by your participation in the faculty lamp-lighting ceremony.
